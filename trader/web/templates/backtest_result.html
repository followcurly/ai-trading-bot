Assumptions
  • buy_hold: red if close[T] < close[T−N]; buy underweight sleeves; hold. Lookback N only for this plan.
  • dca: split starting cash evenly across the first N months into sleeve primaries, then hold.
  • lump_sum: invest all cash on day one into sleeve primaries, then hold.
  • year-end skim (optional add-on): on the last trading day of each calendar year, sell a % of every holding for cash. Works with any plan above.
  • Default fill at next session open (no look-ahead). Same-close fill is optimistic.
  • Cost haircut applied per fill (bps). Benchmark is 100% SPY buy & hold from the first open.
  • Last 30% of the selected range is tagged out-of-sample. A positive curve is not proof of edge.
  • Bars: Alpaca IEX daily, adjusted. ETF universe has low survivorship risk vs single names, but history length still varies by ticker.
