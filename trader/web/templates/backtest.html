Invest equal monthly slices into your sleeves over the DCA length below, then hold and watch for the rest of the range.

Red = price finished lower than N trading days ago. On those days the bot buys a slice into underweight sleeves. Longer lookback needs a bigger multi-day drop before it buys. Only for buy-the-dip — not DCA or lump sum.

Optional for any plan: once a year after you’re invested (lump sum / buy-the-dip: from start; DCA: after the DCA months finish), sell a % of every holding for cash. Not a fixed Dec 31 withdrawal.

Gain gate: skim only when invested + free cash is up by the skim size vs the last successful skim (or invest-anchor baseline). Idle skim/yield cash is ignored. Skipped years keep the same baseline.
